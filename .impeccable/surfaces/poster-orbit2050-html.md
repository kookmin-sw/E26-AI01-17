# Surface brief: ORBIT 2050 A0 poster

Scope: one A0 portrait print poster (poster/orbit2050.html → PDF). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: 22 other student teams in the same room, professors, judges and mentors at the 11/6 presentation and 11/13 exhibition.
Job: understand in 30 seconds that ORBIT moves AI training compute into orbit because the ground grid is full; believe it through cited figures; see the open questions honestly.
Constraints: course order ①–⑥, vector only (no gradients/patterns), CMYK, text ≥40 mm from sheet edge, no disclaimer or version labels.
Memorable moment: the red, overloaded 수도권 bus beside a blue ORBIT bus in orbit, joined by a laser link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The poster is a single-line diagram (단선결선도) of AI's power problem, and ORBIT is drawn as a new bus in orbit. It refuses the navy space poster with satellite art and rounded cards.
OWN-WORLD: Cool drawing-sheet white, black line ink, overload red, ORBIT blue. Square corners, zone-marked drawing frame, title block, revision table, HOLD marks for open items. Gothic A1 for Korean, Barlow Condensed for technical labels and figures.
STORY: The viewer sees the 수도권 bus overloaded, sees ORBIT carry training load in orbit and send back only the model, trusts it through sourced figures, then judges the HOLD items.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and subtitle top-left at display scale; full-width diagram below with a ground layer (generator, transmission line, red 수도권 bus with AI loads) and an orbit layer (blue ORBIT bus, solar arrays, compute), steps 1–3 as callouts on the links; a revision table of predictable 2050 topics beside the title.
FORM: Power-grid single-line diagram, rank 1 of 7 (the pick card), seed d031088d.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
